# Short usability test — MyLifeMirror (MVP)

**About 20 minutes.** Short, English version of the full script ([01_Guion_Test_Usabilidad.md](01_Guion_Test_Usabilidad.md), in Spanish), keeping only the general questions. Main goal: does the app feel **calm and non-judgemental** (RN-04), and can someone use it without help?

---

## Before she arrives (you)

- [ ] Use her phone if possible (the app is mobile-first). Open the app URL.
- [ ] **English:** she can switch it herself with the **EN** button at the top right of the landing page (a good first sign of whether it's easy to find) — or switch it for her before she starts. The choice is remembered on that phone.
- [ ] Check Supabase is not paused (dashboard).
- [ ] Have a timer ready (your phone) and this page open to take notes.

## Say this

> "Thanks for helping. This is an app to check how you're doing in different areas of your life. I'm testing the app, not you — if something is confusing, that's the app's fault. Criticism helps me more than compliments. Try to think out loud while you use it. I won't help much. You can make up your answers; we'll delete the account at the end."

## Tasks

Read each one as written. Don't point at buttons; if she asks "what now?", reply "what would you do?".

| # | Read this | What success looks like | Notes (done ✅ / with help 🟡 / not done ❌, what she said) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Create an account, then tell the app how you're doing right now in about five areas of your life. Include sport." ⏱️ *time the part from opening the interview to saving* | Account created; five categories scored and saved (sport asks for consent). **Goal: under 2 minutes** for the scoring. | |
| 2 | "Go back to the main screen. According to the app, how are you doing? What's your best area and your worst?" | Finds both in the balance chart or the cards. | |
| 3 | "Does the app suggest anything for one of your areas?" *(If all her scores were 5 or higher, skip it and ask again after task 4.)* | Finds and opens "An idea for you" on a low area. | |
| 4 | "Imagine a month has passed. Update two areas — make one of them go down — and check how it changed." | Updates and saves; sees the change (the two answers are from the same day, so in the chart they fall on the same spot — note if that confuses her). | |
| 5 | "You don't want to use the app anymore and want nothing of yours left. Do it." | Deletes the account (asks for her password). | |

Keep an eye out, in any task, for moments where she looks **uneasy, judged or watched** — write down exactly what she said.

## Afterwards — 5 quick questions

"From 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree):"

| Question | 1-5 |
|---|---|
| The app was easy to use. | |
| I never felt judged by my answers. | |
| I felt comfortable answering honestly, even about areas where I'm not doing well. | |
| The ideas the app suggested felt kind and useful. | |
| I'd come back in a few weeks to update my answers. | |

And two open ones:

1. "Was there any moment you felt uncomfortable, watched or judged?"
2. "What's the first thing you'd change? What would you tell a friend before she tried it?"

## After she leaves (you)

- Copy the notes into the results sheet ([02_Resultados_Test_Usabilidad.md](02_Resultados_Test_Usabilidad.md)) the same day — leave the SUS section empty (not used in the short version).
- Rate each problem 0-4 (0 not a problem · 1 cosmetic · 2 minor · 3 major · 4 blocker). **Anything about feeling judged or watched counts as at least 3.** 3-4 get fixed before the Cycle 1 deadline.
